# Customer Interview Fixture

Date: 2026-07-28

Participants: Researcher, Customer

Researcher: What happens after your team finishes a meeting?

Customer: We usually have three different versions of notes. The action items are spread across chat, email, and memory.

Researcher: What do you do today?

Customer: I paste notes into a document and manually write a summary. It takes too long, so I skip it when the day gets busy.

Researcher: What would make this useful?

Customer: I would want it to separate real decisions from ideas. I do not want AI pretending something was agreed when it was just discussed.

Researcher: Any concern?

Customer: Yes. I would not use it if transcripts appeared in logs or if it automatically sent emails without review.
